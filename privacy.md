# Privacy Policy

Furigana for Google Sheets
Last updated: October 31, 2026

## What the add-on reads

- The text in the cells you select, only when you click **Add furigana** or **Remove furigana**.

It does not read other cells, other spreadsheets, your email, or your contacts.

## Where your data goes

When you click **Add furigana**, the selected text is sent to the add-on's own furigana server on Google Cloud. The server looks up the readings in a Japanese dictionary and sends them back. No AI is used.

Nothing is sent to anyone else. No third party receives your text, and your text is never used to train AI or machine learning models.

**Remove furigana** works inside your spreadsheet. It sends nothing.

## How your data is protected

- Your text is sent over an encrypted connection (HTTPS).
- The server only answers requests from the add-on. Other requests are refused.
- The server works on your text in memory and forgets it as soon as the readings are sent back. It does not save or log your text.
- Google Cloud keeps basic records of each request, such as the time and whether it worked, for up to 30 days. These records do not include your text.
- Only the developer can manage the server.

## What is stored

- The readings are written into your spreadsheet.
- A count of how many characters you converted today is saved in your own Google account, to apply a daily limit. Only the number is saved, not your text.

The developer does not collect, store, see, share, or sell any of your data.

## Google user data

The use of raw or derived user data received from Workspace APIs will adhere to the [Google User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including the Limited Use requirements.

## Removing your data

To stop the add-on, uninstall it, or remove its access under **Third-party apps & services** at myaccount.google.com.

# Privacy Policy

Furigana for Google Sheets
Last updated: October 17, 2026

## What the add-on reads

- The text in the cells you select, only when you click **Add furigana** or **Remove furigana**.
- Your Gemini API key, which you enter yourself.

It does not read other cells, other spreadsheets, your email, or your contacts.

## Where your data goes

When you click **Add furigana**, the selected text is sent to Google's Gemini API to get the readings. It goes with your own API key. Google's [Gemini API terms](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/terms) apply to that request.

Nothing is sent anywhere else. The add-on has no server of its own.

## What is stored

- Your Gemini API key is saved in your own Google account using Apps Script user properties. Only you can see it.
- The readings are written into your spreadsheet.

The developer does not collect, store, see, or sell any of your data.

## Removing your data

To stop the add-on, uninstall it, or remove its access under **Third-party apps & services** at myaccount.google.com.
